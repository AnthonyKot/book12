## Provenance caveats (gating)

1. **UK re-typeset**: uk-2013-lifeintheuk-retypeset.pdf is a third-party (Britizen/
   mkdocs) re-typesetting of the Crown-copyright text, supplied by the user. Discovery
   and drafting only; **every UK quote eye-checked against the TSO original before a
   chapter ships.** Optional: buy the TSO ebook for verification.
2. **FR no text layer**: scanned images only; local tesseract has only `eng`
   traineddata. **FR quotes are transcribed from rendered pages and eye-checked;
   never grep-verified.** Page images are the source of record.
3. **US version history**: we hold the 2008 version (rev. 01/19, recovered via
   Wayback — provenance in manifest-agy.md). The current 2025 128q set and M-638
   booklet sit behind USCIS's firewall: manual browser download wanted for ch. 8.
4. **CA/AU editions**: CA is the 2012 text in a 2021 reprint (oath still to
   Elizabeth II); AU is the 2020 trimmed "testable" edition — comparisons must not
   treat these as fresh 2021/2024 compositions.
5. **EE English edition** carries uncorrected errors ("undetermised", "the secret
   largest group") — quote them as printed, [sic], per book8's errors-are-data rule.
